What we need from you

  • Approve the style guide: colours, type, components and states.
  • Vari-Pro raspberry (#E42054): can it be used as a full-width band, or only in small amounts?
  • Typeface: Archivo is provisional. Keep it, or tell us if there's a brand font to use.
  • Warranty: does the 5-year warranty cover non-powered carts too?
  • Photography: which catalogue photos are real, and can you send originals and the product line drawings?
  • Contact and legal details: registered company name and number, and quote response time.
  • Open Vari-Cart renders: for 2 × 8 to 2 × 11 they show 2 to 4 deep baskets per side, but the standard spec is one at the bottom. Can you supply renders that match?
  • Doors: can doors be fitted to an open cart later, or only chosen when ordering? Is the solid door always white Trespa®?
  • Product List: the photo in the yellow band shows the 500 series (single width), but the page lists the 1000 series (double width). Do you have a double-width photo, ideally without the title on it?
  • Enclosed carts' storage: the door pages list 5 trays + 2 baskets per side at 2 × 9 and 5 + 3 at 2 × 11 (the open cart has 7 + 1 and 9 + 1); FW3200-4 EWD lists 2 trays in all, which doesn't fill the height. Which is right?
