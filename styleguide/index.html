Rules

  • Body text is ink or Neutral 600 on white and primer; white or Neutral 300 on ink.
  • Interactive colour is Cyan Deep on light surfaces and Freeway Cyan focus rings on ink.
  • One bold colour band per page: Freeway Cyan or the page's family colour.
  • Finish colours never carry interface meaning.

Type

Archivo semi-expanded for display and headings (provisional), IBM Plex Sans for reading and Plex Mono for codes and figures. Display sizes are fluid; body copy never drops below 16px.
