Why the Vari-Cart

Built for the ward. Repaired on it.

Designed and made in Berkshire, with parts you can swap by hand.

    wide, 540 mm deep

  • 8

    heights, 2 × 4 to 2 × 11 tray spaces

  • 8

  • 75 mm

    castors, two of them braked

  • 0

Storage

Trays you can see into. A deep basket below.

Every height comes with clear SAN trays for small items you need to see at a glance, and a deep metal basket at the bottom of each side for bulkier stock.

Runners have a thick pure-nylon coating, so trays and baskets glide and can't be pulled out by accident.

Tool-free

Repair it on the ward.

The worktop and the runner panels each lift off with two thumb-operated fixings. Replace or upgrade a part in minutes, without tools or an engineer's visit.

Accessories

A medical rail on both sides.

Anodised aluminium universal rails run down the left and right sides. They take our hook-over accessories and existing equipment fitted with universal rail clamps.

Colour

Eight colours, at no extra cost.

Frame and worktop come in Blue, Purple, Pink, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green or White. Use colour to match a room, or to tell one cart's job from another's at a glance.
